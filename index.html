Hi, I'm Ali. I'm a project manager with background in financial mathematics and data analysis with a deep passion for solving complex problems through data visualization and statistical methods. Over the course of my career, I've had the opportunity to wear several different hats—ranging from climate research and statistical programming to academic teaching and technical project management.

Whether I'm managing the launch of a web application platform or diving deep into data analysis, I love bringing clarity, structure, and reliable solutions to challenging technical environments.

Academic and Research Experience

Research Assistant, Canadian Centre for Climate Change and Adaptation

Summers 2021, 2022, and 2024

During the summers of 2021, 2022, and 2024, I worked as a Research Assistant focusing on international cooperation among G7 countries. By analyzing United Nations Sustainable Development Goal data, I applied a variety of quantitative techniques such as LASSO, Ridge regression, and principal component analysis to draw meaningful insights. It was highly rewarding to present our findings at the 25th International Congress on Insurance: Mathematics and Economics, and to have our resulting research published in Nature Communications.
