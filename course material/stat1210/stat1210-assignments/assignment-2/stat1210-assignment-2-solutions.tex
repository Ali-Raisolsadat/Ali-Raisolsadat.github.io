\section*{Question 6 \hfill [10 marks]}

Let \(D\) denote that a plant carries the pathogen and \(D^c\) denote that it does not. Let \(+\) denote a positive test result and \(-\) denote a negative test result.

The given probabilities are
$$P(D)=0.06 \quad P(D^c)=0.94 \quad P(+\mid D)=0.92 \quad P(-\mid D)=0.08 \quad P(-\mid D^c)=0.95 \quad P(+\mid D^c)=0.05$$

\textbf{1. [4 marks]}

Of \(10000\) plants $10000(0.06)=600$ are expected to carry the pathogen and $10000(0.94)=9400$ are expected not to carry the pathogen. 

$E[\text{true positives}] = 10000P(D)P(+\mid D)=10000(0.06)(0.92)=\boxed{552}$

$E[\text{false negatives}] =10000P(D)P(-\mid D)=10000(0.06)(0.08)=\boxed{48}$

$E[\text{false positives}] =10000P(D^c)P(+\mid D^c)=10000(0.94)(0.05)=\boxed{470}$

$E[\text{true negatives}] =10000P(D^c)P(-\mid D^c)=10000(0.94)(0.95)=\boxed{8930}$

\textbf{2. [1 mark]}

Using the law of total probability
$$P(+)=P(+\mid D)P(D)+P(+\mid D^c)P(D^c)=(0.92)(0.06)+(0.05)(0.94)=\boxed{0.1022}$$

\textbf{3. [1 mark]}

$$P(D\mid +)=\frac{P(+\mid D)P(D)}{P(+)}=\frac{(0.92)(0.06)}{0.1022}=\boxed{0.540117}$$

\textbf{4. [2 marks]}
$$P(D\mid -)=\frac{P(-\mid D)P(D)}{P(-\mid D)P(D)+P(-\mid D^c)P(D^c)}=\frac{(0.08)(0.06)}{(0.08)(0.06)+(0.95)(0.94)}=\boxed{0.005346}$$

\textbf{5. [2 marks]}

Sensitivity is $P(+\mid D)=0.92$ whereas Part 4 asks for $P(D\mid -)$. These are different conditional probabilities, so the sensitivity is not the answer to Part 4. The prevalence is only \(6\%\), so plants without the pathogen are much more common than plants with the pathogen. As a result, even a relatively small false-positive rate produces many false positives. Therefore, a positive result has $P(D\mid +)\approx0.540$ rather than a probability close to the \(92\%\) sensitivity.

A Poisson model is appropriate because defects are counted over a fixed length, with a stable rate and independent counts in non-overlapping lengths.

For \(250\) metres
$$\lambda=2.4\left(\frac{250}{100}\right)=\boxed{6}$$

$$\boxed{Y\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(6)}$$

In R
\begin{lstlisting}
dpois(4, lambda = 6)
ppois(2, lambda = 6)
1 - dpois(0, lambda = 6)
\end{lstlisting}

Therefore
$$\boxed{P(Y=4)=0.133853}$$
$$\boxed{P(Y\le2)=0.061969}$$
$$\boxed{P(Y\ge1)=0.997521}$$
